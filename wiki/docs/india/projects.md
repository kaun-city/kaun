## Reading these tables

- **Sanctioned** is the originally-approved cost; **latest cost** is the most recent revision
  MoSPI reports. The overrun is the difference, and it is a *reported* difference, not an audit.
- **Schedule** compares the original commissioning month with the current one. A negative slip —
  finishing earlier than first promised — does occur and is shown as such rather than clamped to zero.
- A dash means the source does not carry that field for that project in that month.
- Projects spanning more than one state carry no state code by design, and are shown as multi-state
  rather than filed under a guessed one.
