# Bengaluru Suburban Rail Corridor 2 (Mallige Line)

**Status:** Delayed

**Route:** Suburban Rail Corridor 2 · Baiyappanahalli to Chikkabanavara

**Responsible agency:** Rail Infrastructure Development Company (Karnataka) Limited (K-RIDE)

**Latest public target:** Dec 2028

**Record reviewed:** 16 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/bsrp-corridor-2-mallige-line)

## Summary

Corridor 2 (the Mallige Line, 25.01 km) is the only one of Bengaluru's four suburban rail corridors whose land acquisition is complete, according to a July 2026 Lok Sabha answer. Its original civil contractor, Larsen & Toubro, terminated its contract in July 2025; K-RIDE disputes the termination, the claims are in arbitration, and the remaining civil work was re-tendered in three packages. K-RIDE now targets first services by December 2028. The terminated contract's value, milestone schedule and payment history were not found in the public sources reviewed.

**Open accountability gap:** The terminated civil contract's value, milestones and payments are not public, so neither L&T's ₹505 crore claim nor K-RIDE's ₹57 crore guarantee encashment can be checked against the contract.

The original scheduled completion time was 27 months, later extended to 30 September 2026. The contract value was not stated in the public sources reviewed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-2025-11], [The Hindu BusinessLine (PTI)][hindubusinessline-2025-08] |
| March 2025 | **L&T halts work citing non-availability of land.** L&T halted work on Corridor-2 and Corridor-4 from around March 2025, saying its machinery and manpower sat idle because K-RIDE had not handed over government and private land, cleared encroachments, or shifted utilities. K-RIDE later maintained that 84% of the Corridor-2 alignment had been made available and blamed L&T for failing to mobilise resources and finalise designs. | Conflicting figures | [The Indian Express][ie-2025-10], [The Times of India][toi-2025-08], [The Hindu BusinessLine (PTI)][hindubusinessline-2025-08] |
| 31 July 2025 | **L&T terminates Corridor-2 and Corridor-4 contracts; K-RIDE calls it illegal.** L&T terminated both contracts on 31 July 2025 (notice dated 28 July 2025), accusing K-RIDE of failing to provide land and work fronts and demanding ₹505 crore in dues for completed work and losses. L&T claimed about 22% of scope was done with 96% of scheduled time elapsed. K-RIDE called the termination 'unilateral' and 'illegal', said the contract did not permit it, and said L&T had made unreasonable demands (contract price revision, converting the EPC contract to an itemised BoQ contract). The claims were referred to an Amicable Settlement Committee. | Conflicting figures | [The Hindu BusinessLine (PTI)][hindubusinessline-2025-08], [The Times of India][toi-2025-08], [The Times of India][toi-2026-01] |
| 6 October 2025 | **K-RIDE decides to re-tender balance Corridor-2 civil works.** K-RIDE announced it would invite fresh tenders for the remaining Corridor-2 civil works, on the advice of the Karnataka Advocate General and its tender evaluation committee, while arbitration with L&T continued. The balance works were split into three packages. | Attributed report | [The Indian Express][ie-2025-10] |
| 4 November 2025 | **Fresh tenders invited for Mallige Line in three packages.** K-RIDE invited fresh tenders: Package 1 — 7.795-km elevated viaduct between Benniganahalli and Shettihalli plus a 1.20-km double-decker viaduct (flyover at Level 1, BSRP tracks at Level 2) between Lottegollahalli and Yeshwanthpur, with Mathikere and Benniganahalli stations, 24-month deadline; Package 2 — 11.569-km at-grade section between Benniganahalli-Banaswadi and Banaswadi-Hebbal with Nagavara and Kanakanagar station boxes, 18 months; Package 3 — 5.8-km at-grade section between Yeshwanthpur and Chikkabanavara with a 185-metre elevated stretch, 18 months. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-2025-11] |
| 9 January 2026 | **Commercial court dismisses L&T plea to stop bank-guarantee invocation.** A Bengaluru city commercial court dismissed L&T's plea to restrain K-RIDE from invoking bank guarantees (a ₹28.6 crore mobilisation guarantee and a ₹31.2 crore performance guarantee, pertaining to Corridor-4). The court noted that despite work-front availability L&T could not show progress and had itself terminated the contract unilaterally, gave L&T liberty to approach the arbitral tribunal, and vacated interim protection from 24 January 2026. | Attributed report | [The Times of India][toi-2026-01] |
| 13 March 2026 | **Project cost revised from ₹15,767 crore to ₹23,342 crore.** K-RIDE MD Lakshman Singh told Moneycontrol the BSRP cost had escalated about 48% to ₹23,342 crore, citing inflation and the switch from a PPP lease model to direct procurement of rolling stock (about ₹4,300 crore). The overall project deadline was extended from October 2026 to March 2030. | Attributed report | [Moneycontrol][moneycontrol-2026-03] |
| 4 April 2026 | **Arbitral tribunal refuses L&T injunction; K-RIDE encashes ₹57 crore bank guarantees.** An arbitral tribunal of retired Supreme Court and High Court judges rejected L&T's plea to block encashment, upholding K-RIDE's position that L&T had defaulted on both corridor contracts and terminated them unlawfully. K-RIDE encashed performance bank guarantees worth ₹57 crore (Corridors 2 and 4, issued through YES Bank) on 4 April 2026. Arbitration on the parties' claims continues. | Attributed report | [The Indian Express][ie-2026-04] |
| 29 June 2026 | **K-RIDE targets first services by December 2028, Corridor-2 first to open.** K-RIDE MD Lakshman Singh said first train services are expected by December 2028, with Corridor-2 leading the rollout. Two of the three Corridor-2 construction packages had been awarded and fresh bids invited for the remaining one; station construction had started on Corridors 2 and 4. | Attributed report | [Asianet Newsable][newsable-2026-06] |
| 22 July 2026 | **Lok Sabha told Corridor 2 land acquisition is complete.** Answering Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 583, the Railways Minister said the project (148.17 km, ₹15,767 crore; 20% each from the Centre and Karnataka, 60% debt) had been affected by non-acquisition of private land, slow transfer of state government land and the absence of a full-time K-RIDE managing director, since appointed. For Corridor 2, the answer said the entire land acquisition is complete, contracts for station buildings have been awarded, and the tender for other works has been awarded with work in progress. The question itself cited an original October 2026 completion target. | Verified record | [Ministry of Railways, Government of India (sansad.in)][sansad-2026-07] |
| 6 August 2026 | **The Hindu reports a separate Lok Sabha reply on land.** Citing a Lok Sabha reply to a question by Chikkaballapur MP K. ## Open accountability questions

- What were the original Corridor-2 contract value, milestone schedule, and payment history under the terminated L&T agreement, and what extensions and delay notices were issued?
- What are the awarded contractors, values, and completion schedules of the three re-tendered Corridor-2 packages, and how do they compare with the terminated contract's scope and price?
- Has the revised ₹23,342 crore estimate been formally sanctioned, by whom and when, and what is the corridor-wise break-up including Corridor-2?
- How much has K-RIDE recovered or paid in the L&T dispute beyond the ₹57 crore encashment, and what is the status of L&T's ₹505 crore counter-claim in arbitration?

These are appropriate subjects for a records-based RTI request to the responsible agency. Re-verify the agency's current Public Information Officer before filing.
