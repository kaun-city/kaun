# Ejipura–Kendriya Sadan flyover

**Status:** Delayed

**Route:** Intermediate Ring Road, Koramangala · Ejipura Junction to Kendriya Sadan

**Responsible agency:** Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA), formerly BBMP

**Latest public target:** Nov 2026

**Record reviewed:** 16 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/ejipura-kendriya-sadan-flyover)

## Summary

The roughly 2.5 km flyover from Ejipura Junction to Kendriya Sadan began under BBMP in 2017 and missed its November 2019 deadline, then went through contract cancellation and repeat tenders. Reporting says the Karnataka cabinet revised the project cost to ₹307.96 crore in September 2023, while later public updates moved the completion target repeatedly. The reviewed public sources did not include the operative work order, current accepted contract value and payment schedule, or an official progress report as of the review date.

**Open accountability gap:** The current work order, milestone schedule, payments and a dated official progress report were not found in the public sources reviewed.

## Deadline and scope trail

| Date | What entered the public record | Evidence state | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 May 2017 | **Original work order issued.** Court reporting states that BBMP issued the construction work order on 4 May 2017, at a reported cost of ₹203 crore, with a 30-month delivery period. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-hc-timeline-2021], [LiveLaw][livelaw-fir-direction-2022] |
| November 2019 | **Original completion period ran out.** The 30-month period from the May 2017 work order ran out in November 2019 without the flyover being completed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-hc-timeline-2021], [The Indian Express][ie-hc-second-chance-2022] |
| December 2020 | **Extended deadline also missed.** The PIL petitioner told the High Court that BBMP had extended the deadline to December 2020 after imposing a penalty for delay, and that the work was still incomplete. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-hc-timeline-2021] |
| 29 July 2021 | **High Court seeks a completion timeline.** In PIL proceedings, the Karnataka High Court directed BBMP to state its timeline for completing the flyover. The petitioner said only about half the work had been completed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-hc-timeline-2021] |
| 17 February 2022 | **High Court directs BBMP to lodge an FIR and act on the contract.** The High Court was reported to have directed the BBMP commissioner to lodge an FIR concerning alleged misappropriation of public funds and to take steps including contract cancellation. This entry records the court direction as reported and does not state the outcome of any investigation. | Attributed report | [LiveLaw][livelaw-fir-direction-2022] |
| 8 March 2022 | **State cancels the original contract.** The Urban Development Department was reported to have cancelled the contract and ordered forfeiture of a ₹10.16 crore security deposit. The report said progress stood at 42.83% and directed BBMP to retender the remainder within the ₹204 crore project cost. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-contract-cancelled-2022] |
| 29 March 2022 | **Court permits an undertaking for completion by December 2022.** The High Court gave the original contractor an opportunity to file an undertaking to complete the flyover by 31 December 2022. In January 2023, BBMP invited bids to complete the project. | Attributed report | [The Indian Express][ie-hc-second-chance-2022], [The Indian Express][ie-third-tender-scrapped-2023] |
| 17 February 2023 | **Third tender attempt scrapped over price.** BBMP scrapped its third tender to complete the flyover. The lone bidder quoted ₹205 crore against an estimate of ₹144 crore, and officials said the estimate would be reviewed. | Attributed report | [The Indian Express][ie-third-tender-scrapped-2023] |
| September 2023 | **Cabinet reportedly revises total project cost.** The Hindu later reported that the Karnataka cabinet agreed to revise the total project cost to ₹307.96 crore in September 2023. The exact cabinet decision date and primary cabinet record were not found in the public sources reviewed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-project-history-2024] |
| November 2023 | **Completion contract announced with a 15-month target.** BBMP was reported to have announced a new completion contract in November 2023 with a 15-month deadline. The operative work order and exact commencement date were not found in the public sources reviewed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-project-history-2024] |
| 21 May 2026 | **Completion target moves to October 2026.** GBA's chief commissioner was reported to have directed additional deployment to meet an end-October 2026 target. The update reported 731 of 762 segments cast and 52 of 66 spans completed. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-october-target-2026] |
| 26 August 2026 | **Latest reported target moves to November 2026.** Transport Minister Ramalinga Reddy was reported as saying most work was complete and the flyover would open to traffic in November 2026. No later official completion report was found by the 16 September review date. | Attributed report | [Public TV English][publictv-november-target-2026] |

## Open accountability questions

- What is the file number and full text of the September 2023 cabinet decision revising the project cost?
- What are the operative work-order value, issue date, completion date, contractor milestone schedule and price-adjustment terms?
- What payments have been made under the original and completion contracts, against which measured quantities?
- What is the dated physical and financial progress as of 16 September 2026, including remaining spans, ramps, utilities and road works?
- What penalties, recoveries, FIR details or other contract actions followed the 2022 court proceedings and contract cancellation?
- Which parcels or institutional lands were required, when were they handed over, and what compensation or transfer terms applied?

These are appropriate subjects for a records-based RTI request to the responsible agency. Re-verify the agency's current Public Information Officer before filing.
