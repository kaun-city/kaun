# Civic Projects

Long-running public works documented as permanent records: who owns them, what changed, what remains unknown, and which source supports each claim.

## Active records

- [**Varthur–Gunjur road corridor**](varthur-gunjur-road.md) — SH-35 widening and elevated-corridor work; latest publicly reported target December 2026
- [**Ejipura–Kendriya Sadan flyover**](ejipura-kendriya-sadan-flyover.md) — Koramangala flyover begun in 2017 with a November 2019 deadline; latest reported target November 2026
- [**Namma Metro Phase 2A: Central Silk Board to KR Puram**](namma-metro-phase-2a-orr.md) — Outer Ring Road metro; opening moved from 2021 to about mid-2027
- [**Bengaluru Suburban Rail Corridor 2 (Mallige Line)**](bsrp-corridor-2-mallige-line.md) — civil contract terminated in 2025 and re-tendered; first services targeted December 2028
- [**Peripheral Ring Road (Bengaluru Business Corridor)**](peripheral-ring-road-bengaluru-business-corridor.md) — notified in 2005–07; Package 1 work order June 2026, latest government deadline 2029
- [**Cauvery Water Supply Scheme Stage V**](cauvery-water-supply-scheme-stage-v.md) — water supply inaugurated October 2024; no current completion target for the remaining sewerage and connection work

## How these records work

Project records are append-only. New deadlines, cost revisions, court orders and RTI disclosures become dated entries instead of replacing the previous public claim. Conflicting figures remain visible until a primary record reconciles them.

Real-time citizen research is reviewed before publication. The reviewed question, answer, search date and cited sources then become part of the permanent project record on Kaun.
