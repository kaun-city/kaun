# Cauvery Water Supply Scheme Stage V

**Status:** In progress

**Route:** T.K. Halli–Vajarahalli transmission main · T.K. Halli to Bengaluru's 110 added villages

**Responsible agency:** Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB)

**Latest public target:** Not public

**Record reviewed:** 16 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/cauvery-water-supply-scheme-stage-v)

## Summary

Cauvery Stage V adds a 775 MLD treatment and transmission system from T.K. Halli and was planned to extend water and sewerage service to 110 villages added to Bengaluru. The water-supply system opened in October 2024 after successive deadline changes, but public updates through 2026 still described incomplete connections, sewerage works and legal obstacles at specific facilities. The main evidence gap is a current package-by-package completion, payment and deadline register covering the whole scheme.

**Open accountability gap:** The public record has no single, current completion target for the remaining sewerage and connection work.

| May 2024 | **May commissioning target missed.** BWSSB's chairman had publicly targeted commissioning by May 2024; June reporting said the launch moved first to June and then to the first week of July. | Attributed report | [The New Indian Express][tnie-deadline-2023], [The New Indian Express][tnie-delay-2024] |

## Open accountability questions

- What is the current package-by-package physical and financial progress, including original and revised completion dates, for all water, sewerage, reservoir and distribution contracts?
- What government or BWSSB approvals reconcile the JICA loan/investment figure with the ₹5,500–5,550 crore project-cost figures reported publicly?
- What are the case titles, case numbers, orders and present status of litigation affecting Chokkanahalli facilities and the 25 MLD Varthur STP?
- How many of the target connections are active and receiving scheduled water, broken down by village and service zone?
- Which contractors hold each live package, and what delay notices, extensions, liquidated damages or other contractual actions have been issued?

These are appropriate subjects for a records-based RTI request to the responsible agency. Re-verify the agency's current Public Information Officer before filing.
