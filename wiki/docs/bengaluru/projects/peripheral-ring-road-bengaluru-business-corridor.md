# Peripheral Ring Road (Bengaluru Business Corridor)

**Status:** Delayed

**Route:** Peripheral Ring Road · Tumakuru Road to Hosur Road

**Responsible agency:** Bangalore Development Authority (BDA), through Bengaluru Business Corridor Ltd

**Latest public target:** 2029

**Record reviewed:** 16 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/peripheral-ring-road-bengaluru-business-corridor)

## Summary

The Peripheral Ring Road, rebranded the Bengaluru Business Corridor and now described as 117 km, was first notified in 2005. Its record since includes land acquisition disputes, Supreme Court and High Court litigation, and a quashed environmental clearance. Package 1 (Madavara to Ballari Road) got a contractor in June 2026, against a latest government deadline of 2029. Public figures conflict on the total cost (₹27,000 crore or ₹17,000 crore), on the number and length of packages, and on tree loss. No public record reviewed establishes the original completion date or the outcome of the High Court petitions that officials expected to be decided by mid-2026.

**Open accountability gap:** Landowners say BDA's August 2026 notifications, which state that possession of PRR-1 land was taken in February 2011, breach High Court orders and contradict the state government's 2021 affidavit to the Supreme Court that only 3 acres 21 guntas of 1,810 acres had been taken.

## Open accountability questions

- What completion deadline, if any, was set in the original 2007 scheme sanction (UDD 399 MNX 2006)? Not found in the public sources reviewed.
- What is the outcome of the clubbed Karnataka High Court petitions challenging PRR Phase-1 acquisition, which officials expected to be decided by June-July 2026?
- How much compensation has actually been disbursed so far, to how many affected families, broken down by each of the five compensation options?
- Did the 18 August 2026 possession notifications lead to contempt or further court proceedings, and were they suspended?
- What tree-felling permission has finally been granted, against the 2022 EIA estimate of 36,824 trees, and has BDA formally sought diversion of 2.4 hectares of Jarakabande Kaval reserve forest?
- Which instrument does the reported 27 April 2026 MoEFCC clearance cover, given TOI's report that the PRR obtained environmental clearance in 2023?
- What is the current reconciled project cost: ₹17,000 crore (October 2025 cabinet briefing) or about ₹27,000 crore (September 2024 cabinet and June 2026 reporting)?

These are appropriate subjects for a records-based RTI request to the responsible agency. Re-verify the agency's current Public Information Officer before filing.
