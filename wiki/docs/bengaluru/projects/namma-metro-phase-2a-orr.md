# Namma Metro Phase 2A: Central Silk Board to KR Puram

**Status:** Delayed

**Route:** Outer Ring Road · Central Silk Board to KR Puram

**Responsible agency:** Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL)

**Latest public target:** Mid-2027

**Record reviewed:** 16 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/namma-metro-phase-2a-orr)

## Summary

Phase 2A is a 19.75 km, 13-station elevated Blue Line section along the Outer Ring Road IT corridor, from Central Silk Board to KR Puram, approved by the Union Cabinet in April 2021 together with Phase 2B at ₹14,788.101 crore. Afcons Infrastructure and Shankaranarayana Constructions began civil work in mid-2021 on 30-month contracts; the opening has since moved from 2021 (2016 DPR) to about mid-2027, with October 2026 trial runs skipping the unfinished Marathahalli and Silk Board stations. Main evidence gap: no Phase-2A-specific cost, progress, or contractor delay figures are public — the sanctioned cost and the 72.75% progress both combine Phases 2A and 2B.

**Open accountability gap:** No Phase 2A-only cost or progress figure is public. The ₹14,788 crore sanction and the 72.75% progress figure (June 2026) both combine Phases 2A and 2B.

## Deadline and scope trail

| Date | What entered the public record | Evidence state | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| October 2016 | **DPR: ORR metro estimated at ₹4,202 crore, operational by 2021.** BMRCL's in-house team prepared the detailed project report for the Central Silk Board-KR Puram corridor in October 2016, estimating ₹4,202 crore and operation by 2021. A tender floated in 2018 was cancelled after the cash-strapped IL&FS emerged lowest bidder in all packages. | Attributed report | [The Times of India][toi-2019-12-20] |
| 20 December 2019 | **BMRCL floats civil tenders in two packages.** BMRCL invited tenders for the roughly 19 km elevated corridor with 13 stations: Package 1 (9.85 km, 6 stations plus a 2.8 km flyover at Silk Board, estimate ₹731.2 crore) and Package 2 (9.74 km, 7 stations plus a link line to Byappanahalli depot, estimate ₹594.2 crore). A senior official said a December 2023 deadline was still possible; sources put the revised corridor estimate at about ₹5,300 crore. | Attributed report | [The Times of India][toi-2019-12-20] |
| 13 October 2020 | **Financial bids opened: Afcons lowest for Package 1, Shankaranarayana for Package 2.** Afcons Infrastructure quoted ₹785.25 crore against an estimate of ₹731.18 crore for Package 1 (Silk Board-Kadubeesanahalli), and Shankaranarayana Constructions quoted ₹623.55 crore against ₹594.25 crore for Package 2 (Kodibeesanahalli-KR Puram). | Attributed report | [The Metro Rail Guy][tmg-2020-10-13] |
| 20 April 2021 | **Union Cabinet approves Phases 2A and 2B: 58.19 km, ₹14,788.101 crore.** The Union Cabinet approved Phase 2A (Central Silk Board Junction to KR Puram) and Phase 2B (KR Puram to the airport via Hebbal), 58.19 km in all, at a completion cost of ₹14,788.101 crore. The Hindu reported that the project would be completed in five years if all went as planned, and that land acquisition for Phase 2A was already complete. | Verified record | [Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs][pib-2021-04-20], [The Hindu][hindu-2021-04-20] |
| 26 May 2021 | **BMRCL awards two civil contracts worth about ₹1,409 crore.** Contract PHASE 2A/P1/60(a) to Afcons Infrastructure (₹785 crore, 30 months) and PHASE 2A/P2/60(b) to Shankaranarayana Constructions (₹623 crore, 30 months), covering the full 18.3-19.75 km corridor and its 13 stations. | Attributed report | [Metro Rail News][mrn-2021-05-26] |
| 7 June 2021 | **Central sanction order sets five-year completion.** The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs issued the sanction order for the 58 km Central Silk Board-KIA line at ₹14,788.101 crore, to be completed in five years; Centre and State to fund 20% each, 60% by loans. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-2021-06-08] |
| June-July 2021 | **Civil work begins; both contracts expected complete by 30 June 2024.** BMRCL's own presentation records Package 2 starting 28 June 2021 and Package 1 starting 5 July 2021, each with an expected completion date of 30 June 2024. It also records that tree clearance permission took about 8 months and the GAIL gas line shift took 12 months from project commencement. | Verified record | [BMRCL, hosted by ORRCA][bmrcl-ppt-2023-01] |
| 24 December 2021 | **High Court permits felling 577 trees, translocating 212 on Silk Board-Kadubeesanahalli stretch.** A division bench of the Karnataka High Court permitted BMRCL to remove 833 trees (577 felled, 212 translocated, 44 retained) between Central Silk Board and Kadubeesanahalli, acting on the court-appointed Technical Expert Committee's recommendation. BMRCL had applied in June 2021; a second application for 1,026 trees between Kadubeesanahalli and KR Puram was pending. | Attributed report | [Swarajya][swarajya-2021-12-25] |
| 10 January 2023 | **BMRCL presentation still shows 30 June 2024 expected completion for both packages.** In a presentation to the Outer Ring Road Companies Association, BMRCL listed both civil packages with expected completion on 30 June 2024, contract values ₹785.23 crore (Afcons) and ₹623.55 crore (Shankaranarayana), and the 13-station list for the 19.75 km corridor. | Verified record | [BMRCL, hosted by ORRCA][bmrcl-ppt-2023-01] |
| 14 July 2023 | **High Court disposes of acquisition writs over metro land near KR Puram.** In Royal Heritage Apartment Owners' Association vs BMRCL (WP 16862/2021 c/w WP 490/2023), the Karnataka High Court disposed by consent of challenges to KIADB acquisition notifications of 2021 for land at Benniganahalli village, KR Puram Hobli, abutting BMRCL property. The acquired area was cut from 261.28 to 119.68 sq m, the General Award of 21 Dec 2021 was quashed, and compensation of about ₹2.54 crore was agreed under Section 29(2) of the KIAD Act, with structure valuation sent to the Reference Court. The order does not state which metro phase the land was for. | Verified record | [Indian Kanoon][ik-royal-heritage-2023] |
| 2 October 2025 | **BMRCL tells ORRCA the line will open only by December 2026.** At a review meeting with the Outer Ring Road Companies Association, BMRCL's managing director said the Silk Board–KR Puram line would be ready for operations by December 2026, BLR Post reported. | Attributed report | [BLR POST][blrpost-2025-10-02] |
| 30 July 2026 | **MoHUA tells Lok Sabha Phases 2A+2B are 72.75% complete.** The Union Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs told the Lok Sabha that Phases 2A and 2B (58.19 km) were 72.75% complete as of June 2026. BMRCL said the Silk Board-KR Puram stretch should open by late 2026. The reply does not break out Phase 2A alone. | Attributed report | [The Indian Express][ie-2026-08-05] |
| 14 August 2026 | **Silk Board interchange walkway contract awarded for ₹55.48 crore.** BMRCL awarded the elevated travelator/walkway linking the Blue Line and Yellow Line stations at Silk Board to Milan Road Buildtech LLP for ₹55.48 crore against an estimate of ₹58.99 crore, with nine months for completion. | Attributed report | [Swarajya][swarajya-2026-08-19] |
| August 2026 | **Trial runs set for October 2026 but will skip two unfinished stations.** Trial runs are planned from October 2026 in two segments (KR Puram-Marathahalli and Marathahalli-HSR Layout), skipping Marathahalli and Silk Board stations where construction is unfinished; the turnback section over the BDA flyover past Silk Board is also still being built. | Attributed report | [Deccan Herald][dh-2026-08-18], [Swarajya][swarajya-2026-08-19] |
| 23 August 2026 | **Minister moves Phase 2A opening to mid-2027; full Blue Line by March 2028.** Greater Bengaluru Development Minister Krishna Byre Gowda said Phase 2A, which earlier had a December 2026 deadline, would likely open by mid-2027, and the full Blue Line (including Phase 2B to the airport) by March 2028. A week earlier he had indicated Phase 2A could open by March 2027. | Attributed report | [The Hindu][hindu-2026-08-23], [Swarajya][swarajya-2026-08-19] |

## Open accountability questions

- What is the Phase-2A-specific sanctioned cost and current estimated cost, broken out of the combined ₹14,788.101 crore sanction for 2A+2B?
- What extension-of-time orders, revised completion dates, and delay or penalty notices have been issued for contracts PHASE 2A/P1/60(a) and PHASE 2A/P2/60(b) since 2021?
- What are the station-wise completion percentages for the 13 Phase 2A stations, and the commissioning schedule for the unfinished Marathahalli and Silk Board stations?
- How much has actually been paid to Afcons, Shankaranarayana Constructions, and Milan Road Buildtech to date, against what milestones?
- What is the total land acquisition cost for Phase 2A, and how many properties, if any, remain in acquisition or dispute?

These are appropriate subjects for a records-based RTI request to the responsible agency. Re-verify the agency's current Public Information Officer before filing.
