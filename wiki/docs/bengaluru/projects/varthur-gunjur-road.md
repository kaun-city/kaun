# Varthur–Gunjur Road Corridor

**Status:** Delayed

**Road:** SH-35

**Responsible agency:** Karnataka Road Development Corporation Limited (KRDCL)

**Affected current wards:** Varthur (Bengaluru East Ward 40) and Gunjur (Bengaluru East Ward 50)

**Record reviewed:** 15 September 2026

[Open the interactive project record on Kaun](https://bengaluru.kaun.city/bengaluru/projects/varthur-gunjur-road)

## Summary

The Varthur–Gunjur work combines road widening and an elevated-corridor programme whose scope, cost and completion date have changed repeatedly. Work was reported as beginning on 20 May 2019 with an original November 2021 target. Completion was later reported as moving to July 2025,[^toi-2024] and in November 2025 KRDCL described 2027 as realistic.[^toi-2025] In August 2026, KRDCL set a new December 2026 target and reported that 80% of land acquisition was complete, with 14 properties pending.[^toi-2026]

The accepted contract value, project-specific contractor identity, milestone schedule, payment record, penalties and extension orders were not found in the public sources reviewed by Kaun. This is an evidence gap, not a claim that the records do not exist.

## Land and the court record

In W.P. 25844/2024, owners challenged a BBMP notification for widening Varthur Road up to Sarjapur Road via Gunjur Village. BBMP stated that accepting development rights was optional and that it would initiate acquisition under the 2013 Act when owners did not consent. The court disposed of the petitions on that basis.[^khc-2024]

The order supports two distinct conclusions: owners cannot be forced to accept TDR, and the identified proceeding did not impose a stay on the corridor project. It does not establish a parcel-by-parcel acquisition status for all properties affected by the project.

## Open accountability questions

- What is the tender ID, work-order number and accepted contract value?
- Who is the contractor or consortium responsible for the current work?
- What milestone schedule and approved extensions govern the contract?
- What physical and financial progress has KRDCL certified?
- What payments, delay notices, liquidated damages or penalties have been recorded?
- Which parcels make up the 14 properties reported as pending, and what does the stated 80% measure?
